Our Story

Earl's Snack House started in 2026 here in Basak Pardo, Cebu City. It began with a simple idea to offer affordable and tasty snacks for everyone in the community.


What started as a small setup has slowly grown through support from friends, neighbors, and customers. We focus on serving simple food that people can enjoy anytime, whether it's for a quick snack or a small meal.


As we continue, we aim to improve our menu and service while staying true to our goal of providing good food at a reasonable price.

Our Mission

Our mission is to serve simple, fresh, and affordable snacks that everyone can enjoy. We want every customer to have a satisfying and enjoyable food experience.
